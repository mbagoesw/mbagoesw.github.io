About

What is Sorting Colour Blocks?

Sorting Colour Blocks is a mobile puzzle game where players move coloured blocks into matching lanes until every lane is completed.

I wanted the game to feel simple and relaxing while still making players think carefully about their moves as the levels become more difficult.

This game is avalailabe to play for free on itch.io directly from your browser or download it on your phone.

Inspiration

Where It Started

This project originally started as a small puzzle game experiment while i was trying to improve my game design and programming skills in Unity. Going home after work ive always seen people on their phones playing simple puzzle games like this and thought it would be fun to try making one myself. Other games like Block Blast!, Homescapes, Block Sort and Water Sort Puzzle were some of my main inspirations for the core mechanics and overall feel i wanted to achieve.

Development

Challenges I Faced

01
Making sure levels were actually solvable

One of the hardest parts was realizing that some generated layouts looked correct but were impossible to finish. To solve this, I implemented a BFS-based solver that validates whether a puzzle can actually be completed before the player sees it.

02
Building the hint system

I didn’t want the hint feature to completely solve the puzzle for the player. The system needed to give enough guidance to help the players when they got stuck while still letting the player figure things out themselves.

03
Making the game readable on mobile

Since the game was designed for phones i spent a lot of time adjusting spacing, block sizes, UI layout and touch input so everything stayed readable and comfortable on smaller screens.

Reflection

What I Learned

01
Algorithms can directly improve gameplay

Before this project, I mostly associated algorithms with technical systems. Working on the BFS solver showed me how important they can be for actual game design and player experience.

02
Small polish makes puzzle games feel satisfying

Even though the mechanics are simple, small things like movement, animations, highlights, sound feedback and responsiveness made a huge difference in how satisfying the game felt to play.

03
Difficulty progression is really important

I learned that puzzle games can become frustrating very quickly if the difficulty jumps too hard. Organizing the level flow and pacing ended up being just as important as designing the puzzles themselves.
