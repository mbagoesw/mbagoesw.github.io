About

What is CapingVenture?

CapingVenture is a 3D adventure puzzle game where players take control of a young adventurer named Taka who travels to a mysterious island in search of the legendary Golden Caping artifact.

Throughout the journey, players solve environmental puzzles using different caping powers such as shrinking, dashing and moving heavy objects. Each ability unlocks new puzzle types and traversal mechanics across multiple interconnected areas.

Inspiration

Where It Started

CapingVenture was designed as a result of final project of a program called Studi Independen and was inspired by classic adventure games like The Legend of Zelda and other modern puzzle games.

One of the main goals was combining multiple traversal mechanics into puzzle design. The caping abilities were created to make each level feel progressively more complex while still teaching mechanics gradually to the player.

The project also explored how Indonesian-inspired themes and character naming could be integrated into a stylized fantasy adventure setting.

Development

Challenges I Faced

01
Designing puzzle progression

One of the biggest challenges was ensuring each puzzle mechanic was introduced gradually without overwhelming the player. We had to carefully arrange level order, tutorial sections, and ability unlock timing.

02
Balancing multiple movement abilities

Since the player gains different caping powers throughout the game, it became difficult to design puzzles that felt creative without repeating the same mechanics too often.

03
Team coordination during development

Because the project was developed in a team environment, maintaining consistent design direction between programmers, artists and designers required constant communication.

Reflection

What I Learned

01
Puzzle pacing matters

I learned that introducing mechanics too quickly can make players feel lost, while repeating the same challenge too often can make exploration feel repetitive.

02
Movement systems can define the entire experience

The different caping abilities became the core identity of the game. Designing around movement and traversal created much more engaging puzzles than relying purely on static interactions.

03
Working in a team

This project is the first time I worked on a game project and it taught me alot about the importance of each role and also communication between team members.
